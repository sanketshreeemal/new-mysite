### Sanket on Advertising

*Ogilvy on Advertising* marked my initiation into the world of media and copywriting. Admittedly, I held strong opinions, particularly towards marketing and advertising — my mind repulses them, almost like an allergic reaction. I perceived advertising as a bag of clever tricks attempting to influence consumer behavior by targeting emotion, instinct, and subconscious bait into consumption. A poison.

Enter Ogilvy's book, elegantly altering my viewpoint. It presents a nuanced perspective on advertising, not focused on manipulation, but rather on **genuine education about the product in a creative wrapper**. 

Noteworthy is the appeal of ads crafted by Ogilvy's agency: not rooted in trickery, but in being engaging, informative, and a pleasure to read. Now, I evaluate ads based on their clarity, creativity, and effectiveness. While skepticism towards deceptive marketing persists, I've developed a newfound appreciation for the ingenuity underlying ads that make you go *"Nice..."*

### Core Ogilvy Principles

- **The Single Pitch**: Always provide your customer with one central, irresistible benefit. Expand to secondary benefits only through separate ads once the core pitch is mastered.
- **Deep Research**: Do extensive research on the product. The more complex or misunderstood the category is, the more research is required.
- **Consumer Language**: Study how consumers talk about your product category in real life. What words do they use? What attributes tip their purchasing decision?
- **Positioning**: Simple definition: *What does the product do, and who is it for?* (e.g., Positioning Dove as a toilet bar for women with dry skin rather than soap for men with dirty hands).
- **Brand Personality**: Products, like people, have a personality. Often people choose an image they want for themselves. In fungible product categories, brand personality is the entire differentiator.
- **Make the Product the Hero**: Don't imply your product is magically superior; simply explain its virtues more persuasively, clearly, and honestly than competitors.
- **Moment of Confirmation**: Showcase the moment when the customer uses the product and has that subtle, visible beat of validation (*"Hmm, this actually works..."*).
- **Glued to Heavy Users**: Always design message strategy around your power users. They drive unit economics and brand longevity.
