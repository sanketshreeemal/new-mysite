Learning - Time is a weird concept in India. "IST" is used in a way to express that it is different from other times in that its unreliable. Showing up at 7 for a 5PM engagement is totally normal. It coudl be 6PM, you know you are already late - but you are okay finishing that last reel, getting into a shower for a long relaxing bath and then heading out. 

People have accepted that "this is how things are" but i think the inefficinecy of coordinating 1.4B people that have no sense of punctuality costs the country years in terms of progress and productivity. Two ways - either i accept that this is how things are done in india, absolve myself from implications and take a back seat, OR I continue to hold people to high standards, get dissapointed and hold the stress and irritation within me - but at least get people to be more punctual than before, held to higher standards than before. 
There are reasons why people are late - traffic, unpredictaibilty of life and events etc - sure, but plan for it at least partially. if its peak hours, then leave 10 mins in advance to account for a wild case. Thats what people do in the rest of the world? 
India will never be a china if people dont respect each others time and be punctual - thats a bedrock of coordination and capitalism.
